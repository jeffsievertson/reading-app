# Human letter sounds: sources and licenses

All sources were checked on **2026-10-23** on Wikimedia Commons (file pages, file history, and wikitext license tags). The original files are kept unmodified in `tools/audio/human/`. Their exact download URL, version timestamp and SHA-1 are in `tools/audio/human/sources.json`. Cut points are in `tools/audio/human.py`.

## Rules applied
- Free with no account and no purchase. Commons downloads need neither.
- Accepted: CC0, PD, CC BY, CC BY-SA. Rejected: NC, ND, "personal use", unclear licenses, YouTube, and commercial phonics clips.
- **GFDL "migration=relicense" caution.** Commons shows the Isotalo and Denelson83 files as CC BY-SA 3.0 through the 2009 GFDL→CC BY-SA license migration. That migration only covers content first published **before 1 Nov 2008**. Several of these file pages now hold newer re-recordings or re-uploads (2011–2025) by other users, and those newer versions may not be validly covered. So for every file we use **the original 2005–2006 version** (fetched from its archive URL where it has been superseded). Each of those versions was uploaded by the original author.

## Speakers
- **Peter Isotalo** (Commons user Karmosin / Peter Isotalo), male, native Swedish speaker. Used for B C D F G H K L M N P Q R S T V W X Y Z. License: GFDL, migrated to **CC BY-SA 3.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
- **Denelson83** (Commons/en.wikipedia), male. Used for the short vowels A E I O U. License: `Self|GFDL|Cc-by-sa-3.0-migrated`, so **CC BY-SA 3.0**.
- **Octane** (en.wikipedia), uploaded 2007-07-23 and moved to Commons by File Upload Bot on 2008-06-24. Used for J. License: **public domain** (PD-self).

There is no single free and properly licensed English speaker who recorded all 26 sounds. Isotalo covers 21 of 26 letters. His J [dʒ] is not available, and his vowel demos are not English short vowels.

## Changes made (applies to all)
Trimmed to the sound (and stops cut from words or syllables). Faded in and out. Continuants (L M N R V Z) were lengthened with Praat overlap-add. X = [k]+[s] and Q = [k]+[w] are spliced. Everything was resampled to 24 kHz mono, loudness-normalised to the app's level (−16 dB RMS, peak-limited), padded with silence, and encoded to 48 kbps MP3. The resulting clips in `audio/human/letters/` are adaptations and are licensed **CC BY-SA 3.0** (J: public domain).

## Considered and rejected
- **Freesound.org.** Downloading needs an account, so it is excluded.
- **Current (post-2008) versions** of Voiceless alveolar sibilant.ogg (2024 re-upload), Alveolar approximant.ogg (Erutuon, 2015), and later vowel re-uploads (2011–2025). These carry the GFDL-migration tag but were published after Nov 2008, so their license is doubtful. We used the 2005 originals instead.
- **Isotalo's IPA "Voiceless bilabial/alveolar/velar plosive.ogg".** The license is fine, but they are unaspirated [p t k], which English listeners hear as b/d/g. Aspirated word onsets from the same speaker were used instead (Sv-pol, Sv-tand stor, Sv-kon).
- **Joni's "Aspirated voiceless … stop.ogg" (2005).** Acceptable license, but it is a different speaker. Not needed.
- **YouTube phonics songs and commercial programs** (Jolly Phonics, etc.). Excluded by rule.

## Letters a listener should double-check
R (Swedish speaker's [ɹ]), J (a different speaker), Q and X (splices), B D G (release plus a short sliver of vowel), H (needs a little vowel onset to be audible), and the vowels (a different speaker from the consonants).
